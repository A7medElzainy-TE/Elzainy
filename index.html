Approach

Simple process. Useful output.

Good internal tools should feel obvious to use. I keep the workflow, information hierarchy, and speed of access at the center of the build.

  1. 01Understand the workflow

    Start from the real task, pain point, and information agents need.

  2. 02Reduce friction

    Remove unnecessary steps and organize frequent actions around how work happens.

  3. 03Build clearly

    Create a focused interface with fast navigation and predictable behavior.

  4. 04Refine for daily use

    Improve the experience around repeated use, clarity, and operational consistency.
